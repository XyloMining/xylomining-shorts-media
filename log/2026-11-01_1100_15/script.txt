Three Bitcoin numbers worth remembering. Can you name them? Bitcoin's supply is capped at 21 million coins. The block subsidy halves every 210,000 blocks, and became 3.125 BTC in April 2024. Difficulty is recalculated every 2,016 blocks to keep block time near ten minutes. Transaction fees are added on top of the subsidy. How many did you get?
