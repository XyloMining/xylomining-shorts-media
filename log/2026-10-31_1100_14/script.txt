Two numbers tell you a lot about any miner. Hashrate is hash attempts per second. One terahash per second is one trillion hashes every second. Efficiency is measured in watts per terahash, the same as joules per terahash. Lower is more efficient. Electricity is typically a miner's largest ongoing cost, so efficiency and power price matter together. Profit is never certain. Want to keep learning? Find the link on my channel page.
