Most people only know half of the Bitcoin block reward. The block subsidy is newly created bitcoin. It halves every 210,000 blocks, and became 3.125 BTC in April 2024. Transaction fees are added to the block's incentive value, so the winning miner earns both. The miner who adds the next block earns that combined reward. Only the subsidy follows the halving schedule. Fees are the other part. Did you know about the fees part? Comment below, and my description link has more.
