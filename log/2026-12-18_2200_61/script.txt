In 2010, a Bitcoin block created 184 billion coins. The checking code did not account for outputs so large they overflowed when summed. One block created over 184 billion bitcoin. A fixed client came out in about five hours and rejected overflow transactions. The patched chain overtook the bad one the next day, so those coins no longer exist on the longest chain.
