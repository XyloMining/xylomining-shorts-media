Does the biggest miner win every Bitcoin block? Most people say yes. Many people think the miner with the most hashrate wins every block. Finding a block is a matter of probability. Your chance per block is your share of the total network hashrate. A bigger share raises your odds, but it never decides any single block. Even a large miner can go a while without finding one.
