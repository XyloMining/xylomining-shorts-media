A digital miner is not a machine you can touch. According to a Forklog explainer, a GoMining digital miner is an NFT representing a share of hashrate hosted in GoMining's data centres. Owners are credited daily bitcoin rewards. They do not hold the underlying hardware, and miners are not redeemable for bitcoin. Rewards and maintenance charges move with the bitcoin price, difficulty and hashrate, so net results vary and can be low or negative. Do your own research first.
