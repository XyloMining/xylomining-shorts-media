Every Bitcoin block is judged by just six fields. A block header is a compact 80-byte bundle. It holds six fields: version, previous block hash, merkle root, timestamp, difficulty bits and a nonce. The previous block hash links it to the block before. The merkle root commits to every transaction inside the block. The nonce is a counter miners keep changing while they search for a hash that meets the target. Which field surprised you? Tell me in the comments, and the link is on my channel page.
