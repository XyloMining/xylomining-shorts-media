A GoMining digital miner is not a machine you can collect. GoMining sells digital miners: NFTs that represent a share of hashrate hosted in its data centres. Digital miners are not redeemable for bitcoin. Owners earn rewards, they do not hold the hardware. So the question is what the rewards look like after costs. That is what to check.
