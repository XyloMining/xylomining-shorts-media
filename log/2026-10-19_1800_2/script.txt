Bitcoin miners are playing the world's biggest guessing game. Miners bundle transactions into a block, then hash the block header with SHA-256 over and over, searching for a result below the network's target. The first miner to find one broadcasts the block. Finding it is hard, but other nodes can check it cheaply. That miner collects the block reward: newly issued bitcoin plus the transaction fees in the block.
