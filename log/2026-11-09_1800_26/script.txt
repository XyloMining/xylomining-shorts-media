Bitcoin miners don't hash the whole block. Just 80 bytes. Here is the surprise. Miners only run proof of work on the 80-byte block header. That header holds the previous block's hash, which links the chain together, plus the merkle root of the block's transactions. It also has a nonce, an easy field to change, so miners can try fresh hashes over and over. Every attempt changes only that tiny nonce, and the header is hashed again. Which part surprised you most?
